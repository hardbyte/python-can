Fixed ``CanutilsLogReader`` stopping with a ``ValueError`` on candump log lines that carry a
Classical CAN raw DLC (``123#1122334455667788_C``, ``123#R8_F``, as ``candump -L`` writes them
when the interface reports one). The raw DLC of 9 to 15 is now read into ``Message.dlc``, as the
SocketCAN interface does, and ``CanutilsLogWriter`` writes it back in the same form.
